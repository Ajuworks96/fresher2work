### Full Description (Google Play & Apple App Store)

Fresher2Work is India’s dedicated skill-verification and direct-hiring ecosystem built specifically for genuine aspiring freshers, software engineers, UI/UX designers, digital marketers, and tech graduates.

Traditional resumes are often overlooked or filtered out by automated screening bots. Fresher2Work changes that by focusing 100% on **Proof of Work**. Showcase your real-world projects, live GitHub repositories, Behance/Figma designs, and verified capabilities directly to hiring tech companies.

---

### 🌟 Key Candidate Features:
- **Verified Talent Passport:** Generate your personalized, recruiter-ready profile link (e.g. `fresher2work.com/u/yourname`) complete with rich WhatsApp preview cards.
- **Proof-of-Work Showcase:** Upload real project repos, live deployment links, video elevator pitches, and certified skills without fake resume inflation.
- **Lifetime Discovery Pass (₹499):** Unlocks candidate profiles to active HR managers and tech recruiters across India with zero hidden monthly cuts.
- **Direct Recruiter Connect:** Get contacted straight to your WhatsApp and verified email by companies looking for entry-level talent.
- **Privacy First (DPDP Act 2023 Compliant):** Full ownership over your data, contact masking, and instant account deletion whenever you choose.

### 🏢 For Recruiters & Hiring Teams:
- Filter through verified, pre-screened freshers ready for immediate joining.
